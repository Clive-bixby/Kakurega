The Architecture of Silence

by Elena Rostova

calendar_today Published 2023
category Genre Sci-Fi / Philosophy

shelves Location Digital Vault

subject Synopsis

In a future where humanity has engineered the perfect ambient noise to suppress independent thought, one archivist discovers a fragmented manuscript of complete, terrifying silence.

Elena Rostova's seminal work explores the boundaries of isolation, the structure of sound, and the lengths we go to in order to avoid confronting our own internal monologues. It is a slow-burn narrative that demands focus, rewarding the reader with profound philosophical inquiries wrapped in tense, atmospheric prose.
